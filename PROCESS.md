## How I used AI
I used an AI assistant (Doubao) in these stages:

- Understanding the assignment: I asked it to explain the essay question in plain language and break down the marking criteria.
- Brainstorming: I listed several possible arguments, and together we chose the "judgment over code" argument, which fits my design background.
- Structuring: It gave me a five-paragraph skeleton (opening, thesis, two pieces of evidence, conclusion), and I followed it.
- Drafting: It wrote a first draft in English based on my real experience — the nine-panel storyboard from my graduation project.
- Editing: It helped cut and polish the draft to fit the word count.
I did NOT use AI for: deciding that the storyboard experience was my core example, and the final read-through in my own voice.

## One thing I kept
The nine-panel storyboard story as the opening hook. It is a real experience from my final-year project, and it carries the whole argument better than any abstraction.

## One thing I rejected
An early AI suggestion for an opening line like "In an era of rapid technological transformation..." — generic and empty. I cut it and replaced it with the concrete moment when the storyboard fell apart.
